does in the piece. Third-party sources are CC0 or public domain, except the Rocketbox characters (MIT), the fonts (OFL, Apache 2.0)
and a few models under CC BY; the required notices are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
The models, materials and HDRIs are generated by `npm run assets` from
[`scripts/assets.json`](scripts/assets.json) (see the README).

## 3D models

Source: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), license [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license).
Each model is the Poly Haven **1k glTF**, then processed by `scripts/build-assets.mjs`: dedup, prune,
weld, keyframe resample, textures re-encoded as **WebP (quality 82)** at most the listed size,
**meshopt** compression (`EXT_meshopt_compression` + `KHR_mesh_quantization`). Where noted, meshes are
simplified with the meshoptimizer simplifier: *ratio* is the target fraction of indices kept, *error*
the largest allowed deviation as a fraction of the mesh radius (pipeline default 0.002).

## Made for this piece

Built in code for this project, with no third-party assets; listed so that nothing on screen is
unaccounted for.

| Element | Where | How it is made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instant camera | `src/experience/camera-object/InstantCamera.ts` | A generic, unbranded instant camera modeled procedurally: rounded charcoal body, leatherette band, lathe-turned lens barrel with knurl and a glass element, viewfinder, flash window, shutter button, film slot and strap lugs. Its plastic and leatherette grain are bump maps generated on a canvas. |
| Instant print | `src/experience/photograph/Photograph.ts` | Paper body with thickness and a vertex-shader curl, paper fibre texture generated on a canvas, and an image shader that develops out of a murky green-brown, carries film grain and a glossy sheen, and can become a live window into the world during the transition. The aged variant (the worn print taped to the utility cabinet inside the picture) adds yellowing, stains and a handwritten date in Homemade Apple. |
| The photograph's image | `src/experience/Experience.ts`, `src/experience/transition/PhotoPortal.ts` | Rendered at the shutter from the lens, out of the same scene (1024 × 1024 half-float target). |
| Overhead cables | `src/experience/world/Alley.ts` (`buildCables`) | Sagging spans between the facades (a parabolic catenary approximation), swept as thin tubes. |
| Sky dome | `src/experience/world/SkyDome.ts` | A back-faced sphere drawn on the far plane; its shader samples the real and the dream HDRIs and blends between them, tilting and turning the dream sky. |
| Lit windows | `src/experience/world/Alley.ts` (`buildLights`) | Emissive room planes behind a few windows, each with a point light that spills into the alley. |
| Curtain | `src/experience/world/Alley.ts` (`buildCurtain`) | A sheer, half-drawn curtain in one lit room: a subdivided plane whose folds and draught are a vertex-shader displacement. |
| Corridor behind the giant door | `src/experience/director/DreamDirector.ts` (`buildCorridor`) | Inside the picture only: a dark box seen through the oversized doorway, its ceiling panels receding through the fog — more inside than any building could hold. |
| Building mass | `src/experience/world/FacadeBuilder.ts` | Dark boxes behind the facades so that windows look into interiors rather than through the buildings. |
| Window onto the real alley | `src/experience/director/DreamDirector.ts` | Inside the picture one window shows a live render of the real alley from the opening shot. |
| Post-processing and grade | `src/experience/render/` | Depth of field, affine color grades, AgX tone mapping, grain, vignette and paper sheen in one composite pass. |
| Sound | `src/experience/audio/AudioEngine.ts` | The near-silent tonal bed, and every sound that has no recorded file listed under [Audio](#audio), is synthesized with the Web Audio API. |
